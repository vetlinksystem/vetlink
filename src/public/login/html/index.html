About VetLink

VetLink is the veterinary management system of Doc Ben’z Veterinary Clinic. It connects pet owners and clinic staff in one place — from booking a visit to keeping every pet’s medical history up to date.

Appointments

Book check-ups, vaccinations and grooming, and track the status of every visit.

Pet Records

Consultations, vaccinations, procedures and medications — all in your pet’s profile.

Breeding

Find compatible matches with veterinary clearance and pregnancy monitoring.

Messages

Chat with clinic staff and get notified about updates to your pets and bookings.

Need help?

  • New pet owner? Choose Client Portal and select Create an Account.
  • Clinic staff? Employee accounts are created by the system administrator.
  • Forgot your password? Please contact the clinic so staff can verify your identity and help you regain access.
