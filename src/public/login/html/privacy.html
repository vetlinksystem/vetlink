Privacy Policy

Doc Ben'z Veterinary Management System (VetLink) — in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

1. Who we are

Doc Ben'z Veterinary Clinic ("we", "us", "the clinic") operates the VetLink Veterinary Management System. We act as the Personal Information Controller for the data described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact information: last name, first name, middle name, date of birth, sex, email address, mobile number, and complete address.
  • Account information: your username and a password used to secure your account.
  • Pet information: pet name, breed, sex, size, weight, age, description, and photographs.
  • Veterinary records: consultations, vaccinations, check-ups, grooming, surgeries and procedures, medications, and clinical findings recorded by our veterinarians.
  • Breeding information: breeding availability, breeding type, breeding proposals, veterinary risk assessments, breeding records, and pregnancy monitoring data.
  • Appointment and payment information: requested dates, services, status, reasons for rescheduling or cancellation, and payment method and status.
  • Messages: messages you exchange with other pet owners and with clinic staff through the system.

3. Why we process your information

We process your personal information only for legitimate purposes connected to veterinary care:

  • To provide veterinary services and maintain accurate medical records for your pets.
  • To schedule, confirm, reschedule, or cancel appointments and notify you of changes.
  • To verify pet ownership and prevent duplicate or fraudulent registrations.
  • To operate the breeding match service, including veterinary eligibility and risk assessment.
  • To send you service notifications and check-up reminders.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and professional record-keeping obligations.

4. Legal basis

We rely on your consent, given during registration, and where applicable on the necessity of processing for the performance of our service to you and for compliance with legal obligations, as provided under Sections 12 and 13 of RA 10173.

5. Disclosure

Your personal information is accessible only to authorized clinic personnel — veterinarians, administrators, and staff — on a need-to-know basis, and to the extent required to deliver the service. We do not sell your personal information. Limited information may be shared with:

  • Other pet owners, only when you initiate or accept a breeding proposal or a chat. In that case only your display name and your pet's public details are visible.
  • Service providers that host our system and store files on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where disclosure is required by law or lawful order.

6. Security

We apply organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect your information, including authenticated access, role-based permissions that restrict who may view or edit records, encrypted transport, and access logging.

7. Retention

Veterinary and clinical records are retained for as long as necessary to provide continuity of care and to satisfy legal and professional retention requirements. Account information is retained while your account remains active. When retention is no longer justified, records are securely disposed of.

8. Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to be informed, to object, to access, to rectify, to erasure or blocking, to damages, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. You may exercise these rights by contacting the clinic.

9. Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Please note that withdrawing consent may prevent us from continuing to provide certain services, and does not affect records we are legally required to keep.

10. Contact

For any privacy concern, request, or complaint, please contact the clinic's Data Protection Officer through the clinic's official contact details or in person at the clinic.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the system before they take effect.
